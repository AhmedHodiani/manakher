# Project Summary: Educational Platform

This project is a comprehensive bilingual (Arabic and English) educational platform designed for schools, serving students from Kindergarten to higher grades. The platform centralizes school activities, educational resources, and communication between the school, teachers, and students.

## Platform Core Features
- **Homework Management**: A system to view, submit, and track assignments.
- **Educational Materials**: A resource library where study files and documents can be accessed and downloaded by subject and grade level.
- **Interactive Quizzes**: Timed, multiple-choice quizzes with automatic grading and progress tracking.
- **News and Announcements**: A central feed for school updates, important notices, and events, complete with a community commenting system.
- **Exam Schedules**: Timetables for upcoming exams.
- **Offline Access**: Core content remains accessible even without an active internet connection.

## User Roles and Capabilities

### 1. Students
**What they see:**
- A personalized dashboard tailored to their specific grade and section.
- Lists of upcoming and past homework assignments, quizzes, and exams.
- A library of educational materials relevant to their enrolled subjects.
- The school's news feed and announcements.
- Their own submitted work, grades, and teacher feedback.

**What they can do:**
- View and download study materials.
- Complete and submit homework assignments.
- Take interactive, timed quizzes.
- Track their academic progress and scores.
- Interact with the school community by commenting on news posts.

### 2. Teachers
**What they see:**
- A management view of the subjects and classes they are assigned to teach.
- Student rosters and profiles for their classes.
- All submissions (homework, quizzes) made by their students.
- The content they have published (lessons, assignments, news).

**What they can do:**
- Create, organize, and publish educational materials and lessons.
- Assign homework and create interactive quizzes.
- Review student submissions, assign grades, and provide constructive feedback.
- Post news updates and announcements for their classes or the whole school.

### 3. Administrator
**What they see:**
- A comprehensive, top-level overview of the entire platform's activity, infrastructure, and technical configurations.
- Full visibility into all user profiles, system settings, and role permissions.
- Complete access to all academic content, community communications, and activities across the entire school.

**What they can do:**
- Act as the sole, ultimate authority over the platform.
- Have total control over configuring all system behavior and user-level settings.
- Manage all user accounts, strictly control permissions, and determine what each user can or cannot do.
- Perform all administrative duties required to run a school online, including structuring academic years, managing the curriculum, and unconditionally moderating all content and communications.
